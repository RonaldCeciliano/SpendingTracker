Privacy Policy

Learn how your information is handled when you use Spendly.

Data We Collect

Information you choose to provide may include account details and expense records, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions, where these features are available. The server hosting Spendly may also record technical information, such as your IP address and browser details, when you visit. Avoid entering bank passwords, payment card numbers, or other sensitive information that is not needed to track expenses.

How We Use Your Data

Information provided through available features is used to support your account, organize expense records, and display spending summaries. Technical information may be used to troubleshoot problems and maintain the service. If you contact the operator for assistance, the information you provide may be used to respond to your request.

Data Storage

When data-saving features are enabled, records are stored in the environment used to host your Spendly instance. Storage location, access controls, backups, and retention periods depend on how that instance is operated. Contact the operator for details or to request access, correction, or deletion of your information. No storage method can guarantee complete security; protect your account credentials and keep copies of important records.

Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google's servers to retrieve these fonts, which may share technical information such as your IP address and browser details with Google. Hosting providers may also process technical information to deliver the website. These providers handle information under their own privacy policies.

Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests concerning your information, contact the person or organization operating your Spendly instance using the contact details they have provided. Describe your request without including passwords, payment card numbers, or unnecessary financial details.
